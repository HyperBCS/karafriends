## The list (2026-10-27) and the decisions from Q&A

1. **History button in the top bar next to Profile, and the top-right icons never move.** Home, History and Profile are always in the same three places on every screen; the current screen's icon shows in the accent (and does nothing) instead of disappearing. On the first-visit nickname screen the three are visible but disabled until a nickname is saved. The History row on the empty search screen goes.
2. **Standardize the icon size in the bottom bar.** Every control in the now-playing bar's control row uses the same icon size and tap target.
3. **The now-playing bar opens and closes from a tap anywhere on it, not only the arrow.** Added later the same day.
4. **Tell the artist from the singer, and make the bar look better.** The user meant the display first (the up-next strip under the video and the "Now playing" corner show "artist · singer" as one muted line), and the phone's bar too. Decision: on the display, the strip and the corner show the artist on its own and the singer as "Ken" with a small initial circle (or "queued by Ken" in the corner), never joined to the artist with a dot; the strip gets a cleaner look (one row per song: title, artist, singer badge, wait; a clearer divider; the "Up next" label set apart). On the phone, the collapsed now-playing bar shows the title on line one, the artist on line two, and on the right the singer's initial circle with their **full nickname** beside it in the muted colour, never cut to the initial alone; when space runs out, the title truncates before the nickname does, and a very long nickname ends in an ellipsis.
5. **DAM audio lags the lyrics.** Decision: the user saw this before the mobile round removed the pitch shifter from the key-0 path (44 ms) and hasn't retested. The offset setting (5) ships either way; the two-element "audio runs ahead" change is not planned unless the retest still shows a lag at key 0.
6. **Audio/video offset (±) and UI scale in the display's Settings.**
7. **Guide melody slider always greyed out.** It works (measured in the real app); it was enabled only for an admin while a Joysound song played. Decision: move it to an "Admin" section on the phone's profile screen, shown when the phone is an admin, always enabled; remove it from the playback controls.
8. **If DAM is lagging, switch to the low-bitrate stream.** Decision: for the rest of that song only; each new song tries the high-bitrate stream again.
9. **Remove the proxy settings and the code behind them.** The user runs a VPN.
10. **Light theme, with light / dark / auto on the phone.** The display stays dark.
11. **Readings replace the text, and follow the song to the TV** (added later the same day). M7 put the reading under the title and left search rows in the original script. Intended: the phone's Readings choice replaces the title and artist text wherever the phone shows them (search, queue, history, song page), and a song queued from that phone carries the choice, so the TV shows that song the same way (romaji on the TV if the queuer reads romaji; kana if kana), so guests recognise their own song. Other phones keep showing it their own way. The "both" option goes: the choices are written, kana, romaji. On the song page the other form stays as the smaller subtitle: with kana or romaji chosen, the big title is the reading and the subtitle is the original; with written, the big title is the original and the subtitle the kana reading, as today. (The lyrics-on-the-TV choice for Joysound songs keeps its own "both".)

## How this plan is run

- Lanes: P1 (server) and P2 (remote) run alongside each other. P3 (display) starts after P1 merges, since the low-bitrate fallback needs the second URL P1 adds. Then P4, then the user's check.
- Checks for every phase as in `PLAN-mobile.md`: `yarn typecheck`, `yarn lint`, `yarn prettier --check .`, `yarn test:unit`, `yarn build-prod`, `yarn test:e2e:ci`, the WebKit run for remote phases (`KARAFRIENDS_E2E_WEBKIT=1 yarn test:e2e remote`), a real launch with `KARAFRIENDS_LOCAL_ONLY=1 KARAFRIENDS_HIDE_WINDOW=1`, and screenshots into `notes/ui-refresh-screenshots/p-*.png` for anything visual, reviewed against the design brief (which gains a light-theme clause in P2).
- The Windows/MSIX notes from `PLAN-product.md` apply unchanged.
- **Unattended (the user is asleep):** every app launch is hidden (`KARAFRIENDS_HIDE_WINDOW=1`), local-only (`KARAFRIENDS_LOCAL_ONLY=1`) and muted (`--mute-audio`, which the e2e fixture already passes); no phase measures audio by playing it aloud, opens a visible window, or asks the user anything. Questions go in the report; the coordinator decides.

- **Now-playing bar:** the whole collapsed bar is the toggle: tapping anywhere on it (title, artist, badge or arrow) expands the sheet, and tapping its header row again collapses it; the arrow stays as the visual cue and keeps its `aria-label`, the bar itself becomes one `<button aria-expanded>` so it is one tap target and one accessible control. Then, as decided: title, artist, singer badge (initial circle plus the full nickname in the muted colour) on the right; the title truncates before the nickname does. Expanded, the current song row matches.
- **Melody slider:** an "Admin" section on the profile screen, shown when `useIsAdmin()`, holding the guide-melody slider (always enabled; one request on release as the mobile fixes made it) and nothing else yet; remove it from `PlaybackControls`.
- **Readings replace the text (item 11):** `WithReading` (or its successor) renders the reading instead of the original title or artist when the mode is kana or romaji; the "both" choice is removed from the profile, the helper and the i18n keys (a saved "both" falls back to written). Every component colour comes from the tokens, so nothing else changes; check the source labels, the pressed states, the drag's held row and the slider in both themes. Add to the design brief in `PLAN-product.md`: the light theme's rules (same type, spacing, radius and accent; no shadows beyond the sheet's; the Don't list applies).

### P3: display: now-playing strip, A/V offset, UI scale, DAM low-bitrate fallback (branch `display-polish`)

Files: `src/renderer/UpNext.tsx` and `.css`, `Announcements.tsx` and `.css`, `IdleScreen.tsx` (the idle list uses the same row), `Settings.tsx` and `.css`, `webAudio.ts`, `useSongPlayer.ts`, `JoysoundRenderer.tsx`, `AdhocLyrics.tsx`, `PianoRoll.tsx` (the lyric or note timing read from the video), `index.css`/`App.tsx` (the root font size), `tests/e2e/display.spec.ts`, `settings.spec.ts`, `screenshotsDisplay.spec.ts`, `CHANGELOG.md`.

- **Readings on the TV (item 11):** the strip, the corner cards and the idle list show each song's title and artist through `src/common/readings.ts` with that item's `readingsMode` (the queuer's choice; "written" or missing shows the original), so a guest who reads romaji sees their song in romaji on the TV. DAM items carry `nameYomi`/`artistNameYomi` already; items without readings show as written.
- **Strip and corner:** as decided in item 4: the up-next strip's rows show title, artist, a singer badge (initial circle plus nickname in the muted colour) and the wait, with a clearer divider and the "Up next" label set apart; the idle list and the "Now playing" / "Queued" corner cards use the same title, artist, singer layout, so the artist is never joined to the singer with a dot. Screenshots `p-display-playing.png` and `p-display-idle.png` at 1080p.

- **A/V offset:** a "Display" section in Settings with "Audio/video offset" in ms from −300 to +300 in steps of 10, saved on the display in `localStorage` (like the mics). Positive delays the audio by that much through a `DelayNode` before the gain node (every source). Negative advances what the display draws from the video's time: the Joysound renderer, the adhoc lyrics and the piano roll read `video.currentTime + offset`. DAM's own lyrics are in the video, so a negative offset can't move them; the setting's help line says so. The audio context is created with `latencyHint: "interactive"`, and the section shows the context's measured output latency in ms as information.
- **UI scale:** "UI scale" from 50% to 200% in steps of 10%, saved on the display, multiplying the root font size from M9 (`clamp(...)` × scale). The lyrics renderers aren't affected (they size from the video).
- **DAM low-bitrate fallback:** in the DAM loader, count hls.js `BUFFER_STALLED_ERROR` events; on the third within one song, `loadSource` the low-bitrate URL for the same vocal type at the current position (`startPosition` or a seek after `MANIFEST_PARSED`), announce "Switched to low bitrate" in the corner, and keep playing. Each new song starts with the URL the popped song says. No session-wide memory.
- Tests: unit for the offset arithmetic if it's a pure function; e2e: the offset and scale settings save and the root font size changes; the fallback can be driven with a `page.route` that fails fragment requests after the manifest on a seeded DAM-like stream only if the fixture can serve HLS, else say it needs the party check with a throttled network.
